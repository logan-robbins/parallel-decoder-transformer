Planner-Conditioned Latent Coordination for\\
Model-Intrinsic Parallel Generation}

\date{July 2026}

\begin{abstract}
Autoregressive language models expose one causal token frontier, even when a
requested document contains several sections that could be developed
concurrently. Existing parallel-generation systems commonly create external
branches around an otherwise unchanged model. We instead formulate
\emph{model-intrinsic parallel generation}: a single trained architecture owns
multiple causal frontiers and produces one next-token distribution for each
frontier in every synchronized decoding round. The proposed Parallel Decoder
Transformer (\pdt) retains a frozen shared lower knowledge trunk and replaces
the upper trunk with three independently parameterized physical decoder stacks.
A prompt-time set planner produces three unordered continuous outlines. Each
outline is hard-routed to one decoder as persistent Plan-KV memory, while a
finite product-quantized notes bus carries block-delayed latent messages among
the decoders. The resulting factorization preserves autoregression within each
lane but makes same-round lane tokens conditionally independent given the
source, their plans, private histories, and previously committed messages.

We describe a source-grounded training methodology for long-form historical
exposition. Cited atomic facts receive exactly one owner lane, optional
reference lanes, hard negatives, exact outline assignments, and token-aligned
cross-lane dependencies. The objective combines lane-local next-token
likelihood with unordered plan matching, fact routing, outline progress,
owner/reference/absent write supervision, presentation order, and finite-channel
regularization. A staged curriculum separately tests the oracle-plan executor,
the planner, and their joint operation. We also register causal evaluations
based on plan swaps, plan removal, delayed-message ablation, a
parameter-matched self-only control, exact dependency-token likelihood, and
lane-specific human fact audits. The architecture and evaluation pipeline are
implemented; scientific training and held-out evaluation are in progress.
Accordingly, this paper presents the theory, design, and falsifiable protocol,
not a positive empirical result.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

A conventional decoder-only Transformer represents a distribution over one
sequence,
\(
p(y\mid x)=\prod_t p(y_t\mid x,y_{<t})
\),
and therefore exposes one next-token decision at a time. This factorization is
appropriate when the next passage logically depends on all preceding prose.
It is unnecessarily restrictive when a response can be decomposed into
complementary sections whose local prose is sequential but whose development is
partly concurrent. Long-form histories, operating procedures, technical
briefings, and other structured documents often have this form.

Prior work demonstrates several ways to exploit such structure.
Skeleton-of-Thought first elicits a textual skeleton and expands its points
through parallel calls or batched branches \citep{ning2024sot}. APAR trains a
model to emit fork control structure and changes the attention topology of the
resulting paragraph tree \citep{liu2024apar}. PASTA learns an annotation
language with asynchronous promises and explicit synchronization
\citep{jin2025promise}. These systems establish that generation need not always
follow the final reading order. They do not answer the architectural question
studied here: can the model itself contain several persistent, physically
distinct causal frontiers, learn which part each frontier should write, and
exchange only a bounded latent summary of completed work?

We call this setting \emph{model-intrinsic parallel generation}. ``Intrinsic''
does not mean that all computation is free or that concurrency guarantees a
wall-clock speedup. It means that planning, semantic assignment, frontier state,
and cross-frontier communication are learned components of one model rather
than a controller issuing independent language-model requests. The model
returns three next-token distributions in one grouped forward operation. Each
frontier owns private upper-layer parameters and KV state, so decoder identity
is a physical axis rather than a natural-language role label.

The distinction matters for both open-domain research and closed-loop
deployment. Retrieval-augmented generation adds an external non-parametric
memory at inference time \citep{lewis2020rag}. That is appropriate when
connectivity and an index are available. Our motivating deployment class also
includes disconnected or fixed-corpus systems---for example, an onboard model
trained around corporate procedures, maintenance manuals, or emergency
protocols. In that setting, the source packet and pretrained trunk provide the
available knowledge, and the question is whether the model can internally
partition and coordinate a long answer without an online retrieval service.
The historical domain used in this paper is a controlled testbed for that
question, not the limit of the proposed architecture.

The design makes four contributions.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.35em]
    \item We give a causal factorization for three synchronized, lane-local
    autoregressive sequences. Same-round tokens never observe one another;
    cross-lane influence is permitted only through messages committed at an
    earlier block boundary.

    \item We introduce a concrete architecture with a frozen shared lower
    knowledge trunk, a continuous unordered planner, and three independently
    parameterized upper decoder stacks. Persistent Plan-KV makes assignment
    structural and inspectable; private caches prevent one lane from acquiring
    another lane's token history.

    \item We define a finite delayed communication channel. Every completed
    32-token lane block may publish four 8-bit product-VQ indices. Producer and
    lag headers address the message, while the learned code tuple is the entire
    transmitted payload.

    \item We specify source-grounded data, losses, curriculum, controls, and
    causal gates that distinguish four questions: whether the physical executor
    can follow oracle plans, whether the planner learns decomposition, whether
    plan assignment causes fact ownership, and whether sibling messages help
    exactly where delayed dependencies require them.
\end{enumerate}

The central claim is therefore architectural and falsifiable. The design
creates three genuine causal frontiers and a lawful path for coordination. It
does not follow that the planner will learn useful decompositions, that the
notes channel will be used, or that the resulting prose will be better than a
serial baseline. Those are the experiments now in progress.

\section{Problem Formulation}
\label{sec:formulation}

\subsection{Three sections, not three guesses}

Let \(x\) contain a source document and an expository request. The required
output is a tuple of exactly three multi-paragraph sections
\(
Y=(y^{1},y^{2},y^{3})
\)
and a presentation permutation \(\pi\in S_3\). The reader sees
\(
y^{\pi_1}\Vert y^{\pi_2}\Vert y^{\pi_3}
\).
There is no fourth synthesis decoder and no serial rewriting pass. The three
sections are complementary parts of one answer, not independent samples from
which a best response is selected.

The planner maps \(x\) to three semantic plans
\(
Z=\{z^1,z^2,z^3\}
\),
where each \(z^k=(z^k_1,\ldots,z^k_N)\) is an ordered outline of at most \(N\)
nodes. The lane-level collection is an unordered set: a physical decoder does
not have a permanent role such as ``background,'' ``event,'' or
``consequences.'' Presentation order is predicted separately from physical
address.

Each source fact \(f\) has exactly one owner lane \(o(f)\). A different lane
may be licensed to reference it, but a fact written only by the wrong lane does
not satisfy ownership. This requirement rules out the weak objective in which
the union of three outputs receives credit regardless of who wrote what.

\subsection{Block-delayed causal factorization}

Generation is divided into blocks of \(\tau\) lane-local tokens; the canonical
implementation uses \(\tau=32\). Let \(y^k_{b,r}\) be token \(r\) of block \(b\)
in lane \(k\), and let \(y^k_{<b,r}\) denote all earlier tokens in that lane.
Let \(\M_{<b}\) be the set of dynamic messages whose publication time plus
delivery delay is no later than block \(b\). With a one-block delay, a message
written after block \(b-1\) can first affect block \(b\).

The model uses the factorization
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:factorization}
p_\Theta(Y,Z,\pi\mid x)
=p_\phi(Z,\pi\mid x)
\prod_{b}
\prod_{k=1}^{3}
\prod_{r=1}^{|y^k_b|}
p_{\Theta,k}\!\left(
y^k_{b,r}
\mid x,z^k,y^k_{<b,r},\M_{<b}
\right).
\end{equation}
For a fixed synchronized microstep \((b,r)\), the three current tokens are
conditionally independent given the state on the right side of
Equation~\ref{eq:factorization}. This is not an assertion that the three
sections are globally independent. Their dependence is mediated by the common
source, the jointly produced plan set, and previously committed messages.
Same-round communication is prohibited because it would introduce an ordering
among lanes and destroy the claimed concurrency.

\label{sec:architecture}

single frozen parameter trunk. Layers at and above \(L_f\) are copied into
three independently trainable physical stacks. A prompt-time planner reads
frozen prompt states and produces three continuous outlines. Each upper layer
receives its lane's persistent outline and the currently visible dynamic-note
window through separate cross-attention residuals.

\subsection{Frozen shared knowledge trunk}

Let \(T_{\theta_{\mathrm{lo}}}\) be the embedding layer and lower \(L_f\)
Transformer layers. Its parameters are frozen. For an identical source prompt,
prefill below the fork is evaluated once and the resulting cache is expanded
onto the three lane rows. This removes redundant prompt computation.

Parameter sharing must not be confused with a shared hidden trajectory. After
the lanes emit different tokens, those token rows pass through the same lower
weights in one grouped batch but retain separate lower-layer cache rows and
separate hidden states. The shared trunk is therefore a common knowledge
function applied to three causal histories, not a single state copied forever.

\subsection{Continuous set planner}

The planner runs once before generation. The normalized prompt states
\(
H^x=T_{\theta_{\mathrm{lo}}}(x)
\)
are projected from the trunk width \(d_h\) to planner width \(d_p\). Three
groups of \(N\) learned queries cross-attend to \(H^x\) through a small
Transformer decoder:
\begin{equation}
\hat Z,\hat V,\hat s=P_\phi(H^x),\qquad
\hat Z\in\R^{3\times N\times d_p}.
\end{equation}
\(\hat V\) contains node-validity logits and \(\hat s\in\R^3\) contains
presentation scores. The canonical instance uses \(N=8\), \(d_p=512\), two
planner layers, and eight attention heads.

The planner's three lane groups are trained as a set. The loss enumerates all
six lane permutations and differentiates only through the minimum-cost
assignment. This follows the general principle that set-valued targets should
not acquire meaning from arbitrary storage order \citep{lee2019settransformer}.
Within a selected lane, however, outline-node order is meaningful.

The planner never consumes generated tokens and is not recurrently called.
This prevents a hidden serial planner loop from reintroducing one-step-at-a-time
coordination.

\subsection{Three physical decoder stacks}

Let the pretrained upper layers be
\(
T_{\theta_{\mathrm{hi}}}^{L_f:L-1}
\).
We clone them into
\(
D_{\theta_1},D_{\theta_2},D_{\theta_3}
\).
Every self-attention projection, MLP projection, and RMS normalization weight
has a leading physical-decoder axis:
\begin{equation}
W\in\R^{3\times d_{\mathrm{out}}\times d_{\mathrm{in}}}.
\end{equation}
The three slices are initialized identically but are distinct parameters with
independent gradients. Grouped matrix multiplications advance the explicit
decoder axis in one operation. Each upper attention layer also stores private
keys and values with shape
\(
[B,3,H_{\mathrm{kv}},T,d_{\mathrm{head}}]
\).

This design is materially different from three prompt labels passed through a
shared decoder. A lane cannot recover its assignment from a learned ``meta-key''
inside a shared cache because no such inference is required: the private cache
axis, branch parameter slice, and hard-routed plan memory already determine the
address. The shared coordination modules process all rows with common
parameters, but the Qwen self-attention and MLP path above the fork is
independent for each physical lane.

\subsection{Persistent hard-routed Plan-KV}

Planner nodes are projected from \(d_p\) to a 256-wide memory representation.
At every physical upper layer \(\ell\), lane \(k\) reads only its own valid
nodes:
\begin{align}
\widetilde z^k_n &= W_z z^k_n + e^{\mathrm{node}}_n,\\
A^{\ell}_{\mathrm{plan}}(h^k,z^k)
&=W^\ell_O
\softmax\!\left(
\frac{(W^\ell_Q h^k)(W^\ell_K\widetilde z^k)^\top}{\sqrt{d_a}}
\right)
W^\ell_V\widetilde z^k .
\end{align}
The result enters through a learned gated residual. Individual nodes remain
available as keys and values; mean pooling or a single additive plan vector is
not the canonical path. Plan memory is read-only and persists for the complete
generation.

\subsection{Finite delayed dynamic notes}
\label{sec:notes}

Static plans define intended work. Dynamic notes summarize work actually
completed. At the end of block \(b\), lane \(k\)'s block-final hidden state is
projected to \(u^k_b\in\R^{256}\), split into \(M=4\) subvectors, and quantized:
\begin{equation}
c^k_{b,m}
=\arg\min_{c\in\{1,\ldots,C\}}
\left\|u^k_{b,m}-e_{m,c}\right\|_2^2,
\qquad M=4,\quad C=256.
\end{equation}
The transmitted payload is the tuple
\(
(c^k_{b,1},\ldots,c^k_{b,4})
\),
whose capacity is
\begin{equation}
M\log_2 C=4\log_2 256=32\ \text{bits per lane block}.
\end{equation}
The receiving process decodes those indices through the learned codebooks.
The reconstructed 256-dimensional tensor is a local representation, not a
256-value message placed on the logical channel
\citep{vandenOord2017vq}.

Every note includes non-payload headers identifying its producer and age.
Shared Notes Cross-Attention (SNC) adds learned producer, message-kind, and
log-lag embeddings before forming keys and values. A receiver's hidden state
forms the query, so the same addressed note can be used differently by each
lane. The window retains up to 16 blocks of history.

Writes are atomic at a block boundary. A note published at block \(b\) is
invisible during block \(b\) and becomes readable at block \(b+1\). All three
writes are committed before any receiver begins the next block. Both Plan-KV
and SNC use zero-initialized output projections and closed residual gates, so
the untrained extension begins as the cloned pretrained decoder rather than an
uncontrolled perturbation.

\section{Synchronized Parallel Generation}
\label{sec:inference}

Natural generation follows one canonical loop.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.35em]
    \item Encode the source prompt and run the planner once. Resolve three
    valid plan memories and one presentation order.

    \item Prefill the common prompt below the fork once, expand the lower cache
    to three rows, and pass all rows through their physical upper stacks with
    the assigned Plan-KV.

    \item At round \(t\), assemble one token row per physical lane and execute
    one grouped lower-plus-upper forward. Sample one token from each active
    lane's returned distribution.

    \item When every active lane reaches a 32-token boundary, compute all
    product-VQ writes and commit them together. Build the next block's addressed
    note windows only after the commit.

    \item A lane that emits EOS becomes logically inactive but remains a
    physically present masked row in the packed cache. Other lanes continue
    without falling back to separate model calls.

    \item Decode each lane independently and concatenate the three sections in
    predicted presentation order.
\end{enumerate}

This algorithm emits up to three sampled tokens per synchronization round while
preserving token-level autoregression inside every section. If the final lane
lengths are \(L_1,L_2,L_3\), a serial concatenated decoder requires
\(
R_{\mathrm{serial}}=\sum_k L_k
\)
decode rounds. The parallel frontier has algorithmic span
\(
R_{\pdt}=\max_k L_k
\)
after planning and prefill. Its maximum span reduction is therefore
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:span}
\rho_{\mathrm{span}}
=\frac{\sum_{k=1}^3 L_k}{\max_k L_k}
\leq 3.
\end{equation}
Equation~\ref{eq:span} is a dependency-depth statement, not a hardware speed
claim. \pdt\ performs more parameterized work than a one-stack decoder because
the upper weights are triplicated, and real latency depends on kernels,
memory traffic, accelerator occupancy, cache length, and lane balance.
Hardware changes can alter that mapping from span to time without changing the
causal potential of the architecture. We therefore report synchronization
depth, arithmetic work, memory footprint, and measured wall time separately.

\section{Source-Grounded Long-Form Supervision}
\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Why historical exposition}

Short answers and question-answer pairs do not test the proposed mechanism. A
lane can satisfy them before persistent plans, ownership, and delayed
coordination become meaningful. The first study instead uses historical events
and processes whose source material naturally supports several long,
complementary sections. Every teacher section must contain 700--1,000 Qwen
tokens and four to twelve connected paragraphs. Lists, timelines, biographies
as primary topics, and catalog-like pages are excluded.

Historical text also supplies checkable temporal constraints. A natural
decomposition should not require a reader to understand an undefined event
before its causes or actors are introduced. This is what ``natural'' means in
the data contract: not that a record proves latent coordination, but that its
presentation order and cross-section references obey the document's causal and
expository prerequisites.

\subsection{Immutable Wikimedia source ingress}

Each source begins from one exact English Wikimedia revision. The immutable raw
bundle retains wikitext, rendered semantic HTML, PageAssessments metadata,
reference records, revision identifiers, licenses, and cryptographic digests.
Only a derived representation is model-visible.

The renderer is a strict DOM allowlist. It keeps the article title,
hierarchical content headings, and ordinary prose paragraphs. Inline links
contribute only their display text. It excludes tables, lists, infoboxes,
templates, figures, captions, galleries, maps, citation markers, footnotes,
bibliographies, URLs, navigation, categories, authority-control blocks,
hatnotes, pronunciation, mathematics, code, and edit artifacts. Reference
identities remain audit metadata and are never concatenated into the student
prompt.

Articles are accepted whole; no oversized page is prefix-truncated and no
fragments are stitched. Table~\ref{tab:sourcegates} lists the preregistered
filter.

\subsection{Two-stage teacher construction}

Teacher data are produced in two schema-constrained stages.

\paragraph{Stage A: cited facts.}
For each accepted source, the teacher extracts 18--48 atomic facts. Every fact
contains an exact source quote, character offsets, paragraph ID, and local
reference IDs that must agree with the immutable source record. The inventory
must span at least twelve source paragraphs and four source sections. Each fact
also receives a plausible hard negative that changes an entity, date,
quantity, or relation.

\paragraph{Stage B: joint three-lane answer.}
One request receives the complete source and validated fact inventory and
returns one expository prompt, exactly three plans, all three target sections,
and their presentation order. The teacher generates the lanes jointly rather
than through three independent calls, because cross-lane ownership and
references are properties of the complete document.

Every plan has four to eight outline nodes. A node specifies its expository
objective, owned facts, permitted reference facts, dependencies, and target
paragraphs. Every source fact has one and only one \textsc{owner} lane; each
other lane labels it \textsc{reference} or \textsc{absent}. An
\textsc{owner} or \textsc{reference} label includes an exact quote in the
target section. Hard negatives must remain absent everywhere.

Cross-lane references are optional. When present, a dependency names the owner
plan, owner node, source quote, receiver node, receiver quote, and fact IDs.
The owner paragraph must precede the receiver in presentation order, and the
complete source quote must fall in a generation block visible at least one
block before the first receiver token. Records with zero dependencies are
valid; the data compiler does not invent communication to make the notes bus
look useful.

\subsection{Tokenized training record}

Retokenization uses the exact student tokenizer and appends one EOS token to
each target. A frozen 1,024-dimensional BGE encoder
\citep{xiao2023cpack} embeds every true fact and hard negative. The same encoder
embeds a textual representation of every outline node; a fixed Gaussian
Johnson--Lindenstrauss projection maps those targets to 512 dimensions.
Retokenization materializes:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.25em]
    \item true and hard-negative fact embeddings;
    \item eight padded continuous plan targets per lane and node-validity masks;
    \item exact owned-fact-to-outline-node routing;
    \item one active outline node per 32-token target block;
    \item \textsc{owner}/\textsc{reference}/\textsc{absent} labels at the
    exact lane and block where evidence occurs;
    \item exact dependency-token masks and source-block-to-target-token edges;
    \item the reader-facing presentation ranks.
\end{itemize}

The first pilot targets 128 accepted records balanced across eight historical
categories. Every record is manually inspected. A 2,048-record corpus is the
architecture gate; a larger corpus is not authorized until the oracle executor
and learned planner show value. Four separately curated source-grounded
examples currently exercise the complete schema and trainer interface. They
are data-contract inspection records, not a training corpus or an empirical
result.

\section{Learning Objective}
\label{sec:objective}

\subsection{Unordered plan matching}

For example \(i\), let \(\hat z_{k,n}\) and \(\hat v_{k,n}\) be predicted plan
nodes and validity logits, and let \(z_{k,n}\) and \(v_{k,n}\) be teacher
targets. For each lane permutation \(\sigma\in S_3\), define
\begin{align}
C_i(\sigma)
={}&
\frac{
\sum_{k,n} v_{k,n}
\left[1-\cos(\hat z_{\sigma(k),n},z_{k,n})\right]
}{
\sum_{k,n} v_{k,n}
}
\nonumber\\
&+
\frac{1}{3N}\sum_{k,n}
\BCE\!\left(\hat v_{\sigma(k),n},v_{k,n}\right).
\end{align}
The selected assignment is
\(
\sigma_i^\star=\arg\min_{\sigma\in S_3} C_i(\sigma)
\).
The discrete choice is detached; gradients flow through the selected predicted
nodes. All downstream semantic axes are then expressed in that matched order.

\subsection{Execution and semantic losses}

\paragraph{Lane token likelihood.}
\(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{tok}}\) is exact teacher-forced next-token cross entropy
over every valid token, including each lane's EOS. The first token in every
target block is scored from the preceding prompt or block-final distribution,
so block packing does not drop boundary likelihood.

\paragraph{Owned-fact routing.}
The route head scores every plan node against all true and hard-negative fact
embeddings. \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{route}}\) gives equal aggregate weight to
positive owned node--fact pairs and negative pairs. Reference facts are not
ownership targets for this head.

\paragraph{Outline progress.}
The block-final hidden state is compared with the assigned plan nodes.
\(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{progress}}\) is cross entropy over the node whose target
paragraph occupies most of that block.

\paragraph{Fact writing.}
For every lane, target block, and fact query, a classifier predicts
\textsc{owner}, \textsc{reference}, or \textsc{absent}.
\(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{write}}\) uses inverse-frequency class weights
\(
w_c=n/(3n_c)
\),
preventing the many absent labels from defining a trivial optimum. A fact in
the wrong physical lane is explicitly penalized even if it appears somewhere
in the combined answer.

\paragraph{Dynamic-note alignment.}
For every active block write, the quantized note is paired with its
pre-quantized projection. \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{note}}\) is symmetric InfoNCE
over all writes in the packed document with temperature \(0.07\). Other
lane/block writes supply negatives.

\paragraph{Presentation order.}
Given matched lane scores \(s\) and teacher order
\(\pi=(\pi_1,\pi_2,\pi_3)\), a Plackett--Luce loss is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{order}}
=-\log\frac{e^{s_{\pi_1}}}{\sum_j e^{s_j}}
-\log\frac{e^{s_{\pi_2}}}{e^{s_{\pi_2}}+e^{s_{\pi_3}}}.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Finite-channel regularization.}
For continuous subvector \(u\) and selected code \(e\),
\begin{align}
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{commit}}&=\|u-\sg[e]\|_2^2,\\
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{codebook}}&=\|\sg[u]-e\|_2^2.
\end{align}
\(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{usage}}\) is the normalized deficit between maximum
code entropy and the empirical marginal entropy in each product codebook. It
discourages collapse without increasing channel capacity.

\subsection{Combined objective}

After plan matching, the canonical full objective is
\begin{align}
\mathcal L
={}&1.00\mathcal L_{\mathrm{tok}}
+1.00\mathcal L_{\mathrm{plan}}
+1.00\mathcal L_{\mathrm{route}}
+0.50\mathcal L_{\mathrm{progress}}
\nonumber\\
&+1.00\mathcal L_{\mathrm{write}}
+0.25\mathcal L_{\mathrm{note}}
+0.10\mathcal L_{\mathrm{order}}
\nonumber\\
&+0.25\mathcal L_{\mathrm{commit}}
+1.00\mathcal L_{\mathrm{codebook}}
+0.10\mathcal L_{\mathrm{usage}}.
\label{eq:objective}
\end{align}
These coefficients are a registered starting configuration, not an asserted
optimum. The architecture screen varies learning rate, weight decay, fork
depth, planner capacity, notes width, codebook geometry, and residual-gate
initialization before any selected interactions are tested in a separate
registered run.

\subsection{Fact ownership and long-form quality}

Free generation stops each lane at its own EOS or at 1,000 new tokens. A valid
lane contains at least 700 tokens and four paragraphs, with no more than 10\%
of sentences shorter than eight words. These checks reject the short-sentence
failure mode directly rather than treating token count alone as long-form
quality.

Fact presence is not measured by exact string identity. A pinned NLI
cross-encoder scores each atomic claim against sentence-level evidence units in
each physical lane. Its threshold is calibrated once on disjoint teacher prose
containing true and hard-negative claims, then frozen for held-out generation.
This automatic score is a screen, not final factual evidence. Every
condition--lane--fact decision is exported to a blinded queue. Two independent
annotators judge the complete queue; a distinct third adjudicator resolves all
disagreements and uncertain cases. Final owner recall, reference recall,
unauthorized leakage, and hard-negative rate use those adjudicated judgments.

\subsection{Causal communication measurement}

Teacher-forced communication evaluation reruns the exact target with dynamic
notes removed while leaving persistent Plan-KV unchanged. Retokenization has
already marked every token overlapping a required receiver quote and proved
that its source block is visible under the one-block delay. For token \(q\),
define
\begin{equation}
\Delta_q
=\operatorname{NLL}_{\mathrm{no\text{-}dynamic}}(q)
-\operatorname{NLL}_{\mathrm{bus}}(q).
\end{equation}
The evaluator reports the document-level mean \(\Delta\) on dependency tokens,
the mean on all other active tokens, and their selectivity difference. A global
likelihood change is insufficient: the intended mechanism predicts a larger
benefit at annotated delayed dependencies.

Additional destructive controls mutate one producer's finite code, exchange
note sources, scramble note directions at matched norm, or close the note
gate. These interventions diagnose address and payload sensitivity. They do not
replace the primary bus-versus-self-only comparison.

\subsection{Scale and architecture controls}

The first model rung is a dense Qwen3-4B checkpoint
\citep{yang2025qwen3}. If its oracle-plan executor fails, the response is not
immediately to declare the architecture impossible. Data alignment, ownership
labels, routing heads, optimization, cache behavior, and plan delivery are
audited first. Only if those checks pass is the same architecture repeated with
a dense 14B trunk as a capacity control. The larger model does not change the
scientific condition; it asks whether a negative 4B result is a trunk-capacity
result.

\subsection{Status}

At the time of writing, the three physical decoder stacks, private cache,
planner, Plan-KV, product-VQ notes bus, packed training rollout, counterfactual
runtime, historical source filter, strict teacher schemas, and evaluators are
implemented. Four manually audited long-form records exercise the data and
trainer contracts, including sparse and zero-dependency cases. These checks
establish executable plumbing only. Training on an admissible corpus and
held-out causal evaluation are in progress; no quality, coordination, or
speedup result is reported in this manuscript.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Prompted and learned semantic branching.}
Skeleton-of-Thought externalizes a textual skeleton and expands its points
through parallel requests or batched decoding \citep{ning2024sot}. APAR
post-trains a model on hierarchical paragraph structure, uses fork control
tokens, and restricts attention to ancestors and a branch's own history
\citep{liu2024apar}. PASTA learns a promise-and-sync annotation language so
semantically independent chunks can be decoded asynchronously and later
rejoined \citep{jin2025promise}. \pdt\ shares the premise that final reading
order need not equal generation order. It differs by making three persistent
upper decoder stacks part of the model and by conditioning each on a continuous
plan plus a finite delayed latent channel.

\paragraph{Concurrent workers with high-bandwidth visibility.}
Hogwild! Inference lets concurrent model instances attend through a
concurrently updated shared cache \citep{rodionov2025hogwild}. Group Think gives
multiple concurrent reasoning trajectories token-level visibility into one
another \citep{hsu2025groupthink}. These approaches explore rich and immediate
cross-worker information. \pdt\ studies the opposite communication regime:
private causal caches, an explicit one-block barrier, and 32 learned payload
bits per lane block. The restriction makes causal attribution and channel
capacity directly measurable.

\paragraph{Non-autoregressive and multi-token generation.}
Non-autoregressive translation predicts many target positions in parallel,
trading away the standard left-to-right factorization and often relying on
distillation \citep{gu2018nat,kim2016seqkd}. Speculative decoding accelerates
sampling while preserving a target model's output distribution
\citep{leviathan2023spec}; Medusa adds multiple decoding heads
\citep{cai2024medusa}, and EAGLE speculates in feature space
\citep{li2024eagle}. Multi-token prediction trains independent future-token
heads above a shared trunk \citep{gloeckle2024multitoken}. \pdt\ has a different
target: each output head owns a complete, persistent autoregressive section,
and the three sections intentionally differ from the sequence a single
left-to-right decoder would have sampled.

\paragraph{Set prediction, discrete latents, and external memory.}
Attention over learned seed queries provides a natural mechanism for
set-valued outputs \citep{lee2019settransformer}; \pdt\ combines learned planner
queries with exact three-way assignment. Product vector quantization supplies
a differentiable finite alphabet \citep{vandenOord2017vq}. Retrieval-augmented
generation couples a parametric generator to external non-parametric memory
\citep{lewis2020rag}; \pdt's plan and notes are instead internal,
document-specific state derived from the current source and generated blocks.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Value of physical decoders without constant communication}

The dynamic notes bus is not required to fire on every example for the physical
split to be meaningful. Three independent upper stacks can adapt their
attention and MLP parameters to concurrent long-form trajectories, maintain
private causal summaries, and follow different persistent plans without forcing
all three roles through one upper parameterization. The output is still three
tokens per synchronized round even when the optimal dynamic message is
uninformative.

This does not imply that the stacks should become fixed experts. Random
semantic-to-physical assignment makes permanent roles actively unreliable.
The intended distinction is between \emph{trajectory capacity} and
\emph{semantic identity}: each physical stack may learn how to sustain one
long concurrent trajectory, while its subject matter is determined by the
assigned plan. The self-only and no-dynamic controls measure the value of that
physical factorization; the bus ablation measures only the incremental value of
sibling communication.

\subsection{Why persistent attention rather than a latent seed}

A single additive vector can be ignored, overwritten, or entangled with lane
state. Plan-KV instead exposes each outline node as an addressable memory at
every upper layer and every token. This does not assume that natural-language
instructions correspond to a linear direction in the residual stream. It asks
the model to learn queries into a persistent structured memory. Complete-plan
swaps then provide a causal test: if assignment is real, fact ownership must
move while physical weights remain fixed.

\subsection{Offline and fixed-corpus systems}

The architecture is compatible with systems that cannot query an external
service during generation. A domain-specific source packet can be provided in
context, or a trunk can be trained on a controlled corpus. The planner and
notes bus operate entirely on local hidden states. For operating procedures,
vehicle manuals, maritime protocols, or other closed environments, this could
support concurrent generation of complementary sections while retaining a
bounded and inspectable internal communication path.

This is a deployment hypothesis, not evidence that Wikipedia-trained historical
decomposition transfers automatically to safety-critical procedures. Such a
transfer would require domain data, expert validation, failure-mode testing,
and a separate safety case.

\subsection{Falsifiability}

The design would be weakened by any of the following outcomes:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.25em]
    \item the oracle-plan executor cannot achieve reliable lane ownership,
    showing that three physical stacks do not by themselves solve assignment;
    \item the learned planner remains far below the oracle despite sufficient
    executor performance, showing that decomposition is the bottleneck;
    \item plan swaps leave facts at their original physical addresses, showing
    that branches learned fixed roles or ignored Plan-KV;
    \item dynamic-note removal has no selective effect on annotated dependency
    tokens, showing that the bus is unused or unnecessary;
    \item the self-only control matches the bus on dependency-sensitive held-out
    data, showing that sibling communication adds no measured value;
    \item grouped execution fails to reduce practical complete-answer latency,
    showing that algorithmic span is not realized on the tested hardware.
\end{itemize}

These outcomes are separable. For example, a failed notes bus does not erase
the architectural novelty of physical parallel frontiers, but it does falsify
the stronger claim that a finite latent channel supports useful cross-lane
coordination.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Three independent upper stacks increase parameters, optimizer state, and
upper-layer memory traffic. The canonical 24/12 split is a test point, not proof
that half-depth branching is optimal. Realized speed depends on hardware and
kernel support, and Equation~\ref{eq:span} must not be quoted as measured
latency.

The initial evidence domain is English historical exposition. It is selected
because facts, chronology, citations, and multi-section structure can be
audited, not because history represents every long-form task. Code,
mathematics, fiction, and tightly sequential reasoning may admit different
decompositions or none at all.

Wikipedia quality assessments and citations are filters, not guarantees that
every source sentence is true. Teacher-generated facts and plans can introduce
errors even under exact provenance schemas. BGE plan targets, NLI fact screens,
and product-VQ codes are model-dependent representations. Human inspection and
held-out adjudication remain necessary.

The one-block barrier excludes useful same-round interactions by design. A
32-bit message may be too narrow, while a wider or more frequent channel may
erase the intended communication constraint. The architecture screen can
measure this trade-off but cannot make it disappear.

Finally, implementation tests show tensor shapes, causal delay, parameter
movement, and cache growth. They cannot establish semantic coordination. Until
training and held-out evaluation are complete, no result should be inferred
from executable plumbing or from untrained generated tokens.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The Parallel Decoder Transformer turns long-form parallel generation into a
model architecture rather than an external scheduling trick. A frozen shared
lower trunk supplies common language and world knowledge; a continuous planner
produces an unordered set of three outlines; three independently parameterized
upper stacks own private causal frontiers; persistent Plan-KV supplies
structural assignment; and a finite delayed notes bus provides a lawful path
for cross-lane influence. Under this factorization, the model can emit three
tokens per synchronization round without violating autoregression inside any
section.

The harder question is whether the architecture learns the intended behavior.
The proposed data contract and evaluation separate executor capability,
planner decomposition, plan causality, and dynamic communication. Those tests
are now in progress. The contribution of this paper is the precise,
implementable, and falsifiable design against which the eventual result can be
judged.

\section{Registered Training Configuration}
\label{app:training}

The canonical optimizer uses AdamW with learning rate \(2\times10^{-4}\),
weight decay \(0.01\), 1,250 warmup steps, cosine decay, gradient-norm clipping
at 1.0, batch size one document, and gradient accumulation over 16 documents.
The maximum planner prompt is 16,384 tokens; this is a fail-fast ceiling, not a
padding length. Source documents are never truncated to meet it.

The architecture screen contains 25 one-factor variants at three independent
seeds. It varies model scale, upper-stack depth, planner depth and feed-forward
width, notes and attention width, product-code shape, gate initialization,
learning rate, weight decay, and bus versus parameter-matched self-only memory.
It is compiled before training, and any interaction study is registered after
the one-factor screen rather than selected post hoc.
