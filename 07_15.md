## 0. Verdict

**On to something. Not a waste of time — with one hard condition.**

- ~75% confidence the scientific question and the design point are real,
  unoccupied, and worth continued investment.
- ~45% confidence the strongest form of the claim (the *dynamic* bus carries
  irreplaceable sibling-dependency information at ≤512 B per stream-block)
  survives its own ablations.
- \>90% confidence that executing the falsification contract below produces a
  publishable result in either direction, because the measurement instrument
  itself is novel. The risk is execution, not concept.

### 0.1 The steelman of "waste of time," conceded then answered

Steelman: full-bandwidth sharing is free and training-free. Hogwild!
(2504.06261), Group Think (2505.11107, ICLR-26 Oral), and Asynchronous
Reasoning (2512.10931) coordinate K streams of one frozen model with **zero
trained parameters** by pointing attention at a shared KV cache; on a single
accelerator the sibling KV is already resident, so a 9,216× bandwidth saving
saves a resource that costs nothing at K=3. PDT spends ~400M sidecar
parameters and a 4-stage curriculum to reconstruct, lossily and one block
late, information the training-free baseline gets exactly and instantly —
and the current repo cannot demonstrate any of its claims (dead
agreement/coverage heads, a `sigmoid(mean(hidden))` noise gate, four
train/runtime semantic divergences, a single-template 256-example dataset).

Concede all implementation criticisms — they are §4's work list. Three
arguments survive the steelman:

1. **Memory-bound decoding cuts PDT's way exactly in the long-output regime.**
   Decode cost is memory traffic, not FLOPs. Full cross-stream visibility
   means every stream reads every sibling's KV every token: for Qwen3-4B that
   is 73,728 KV values per token per sibling, so cross-stream read traffic
   scales O(K²·L) and grows without bound in output length L. The bus's
   cross-stream traffic is O(K²·L/τ)·d_notes — 9,216× less per stream-block
   at d_notes=256 (≈1.2×10⁶× less at the 32-bit quantized notes of §5) — and
   the reader-side cost (≤K notes at 12 layers) is **invariant in L**. The
   longer the output, the larger the gap. Off one device the asymmetry is
   categorical: 512 B/block is schedulable over any interconnect and
   delay-tolerant by construction (Δ≥1); a shared KV cache is neither.
2. **The scientific question exists independent of systems value: how many
   bits per block of sibling visibility does coherent decomposition need?**
   The verified field is bimodal — zero bits (Skeleton-of-Thought, Planned
   Diffusion, fork-join decomposers) or ~10⁵–10⁶ bit-equivalents per block
   (Hogwild!, Group Think, LACE, LaneRoPE, Multi-Stream LLMs), with LeaP at
   ~10³–10⁴ bits in *text*. Nobody has measured the rate–distortion curve
   between the poles. PDT is the only instrument built to measure it. Either
   answer — "64 latent bits suffice" or "nothing below full KV works" — is a
   real result.
3. **Convergent independent evidence de-risks the mechanism family.** The
   Bicameral Model (2605.11167, 2026-05) trains zero-init gated latent
   coupling between frozen LMs on CE alone and gets an emergent selective
   protocol — PDT's core training premise, independently validated. LACE
   (2604.15529) shows learned reduced-dimension cross-thread attention
   improves parallel decoding. Interlat (2511.09149) shows learned latent
   messages beat text. None occupies PDT's point; all confirm its
   ingredients work.

### 0.2 The throughput intuition, adjudicated

The founding intuition — *parallel-stream advantage grows with total output
length* — is **sound in a specific regime and must be claimed as such**:

- Hardware half: correct, and *because of* memory-bound decoding, not despite
  it. Batch-of-K decoding reads trunk weights once per step for K tokens, so a
  K-way decomposition of an L-token output takes ~L/K weight-sweeps versus L
  sequentially; the K× prefill overhead amortizes as L grows. Full-KV rivals
  get the same batching win but erode it with O(K·L)-growing sibling reads;
  text-exchange methods pay full re-encodes per round.
- Information half: conditional. The planner must find cuts whose *residual*
  inter-part information rate ρ (bits/block not predictable from the shared
  plan) fits inside bus capacity, and a serial dependency chain of depth D
  needs M = L/(Kτ) ≥ Δ·D blocks of runway. Long structured outputs
  (multi-section documents, reports, multi-file code) plausibly satisfy both;
  dense serial derivations do not — and no parallel method wins there, which
  is why the rest of the field parallelizes redundant *attempts* instead of
  *parts*.

Formalized as Proposition P6 (§3) with a pre-registered crossover length L*.

### 0.3 The most likely disappointing outcome, made publishable

The likeliest bad outcome is not "bus fails" but **"anchors alone suffice"** —
a latent-Skeleton-of-Thought null in which planner snapshot-0 captures ~all
the benefit and dynamic co-referencing adds nothing. The 2×2 factorial of §7
makes that a clean pre-registered negative ("planning is sufficient;
co-referencing is unnecessary at this task family") instead of a wasted year.

## 2. The Headline Claim and Its Kill Criteria

Lead sentence for v3 / NeurIPS:

> **K parallel decoding streams of a single frozen LLM, coordinating only
> through a learned latent bus of at most 32 bits per stream per 32-token
> block, recover at least 70% of the dependency-span cross-entropy improvement
> that full KV-cache sharing provides over independent parallel decoding — at
> roughly four orders of magnitude lower inter-stream bandwidth — and the
> delivered dependency information never exceeds the bus's measured bit
> budget.**

70% and 32 bits are pre-registered points on a rate ladder {32-bit VQ, 512 B
continuous}; the final bound clause makes the sentence self-auditing (§5).
The claim is anchored at both poles the field already owns — independent
decoding (SoT pole) and full KV (Hogwild!/Group Think pole) — so it is a
measurement of the unmeasured middle, not a "we beat X" claim.

Kill criteria reviewers will run at, each pre-empted by a spec section:

1. **"It's just the plan" (latent-SoT confound).** Pre-empt: 2×2 factorial
   {anchors on/off} × {dynamic notes on/off} plus a parameter-matched
   self-only control (§7). Report the dynamic-bus increment as its own
   headline number; a ~0 increment publishes as the §0.3 negative.
2. **"Toy data / memorization."** Pre-empt three ways: procedurally generated
   dependency *families* with held-out families (§4.4); the capacity
   accounting alarm — any ΔCE exceeding transmitted bits is mathematically
   impossible via the bus, so leakage self-reports (§5); one natural
   long-form task beside the synthetic suite (§11).
3. **"Your runtime doesn't match your training" / "teacher-forced CE is not
   decoding."** Pre-empt: unify read/write semantics *by construction* (CRDT
   bus, §4.1), then report end-to-end free-running decode: wall-clock at
   matched quality vs (a) sequential decoding and (b) a properly implemented
   Group-Think-style shared-KV baseline on the same trunk at equal K — the
   baseline is training-free, so implementing it well is cheap and
   unimpeachable (§4.5).

## 3. Formal Model

**Unifying frame (one sentence, and the v3 §1):** PDT is *distributed source
coding plus transactional commit over a delay-Δ broadcast channel among K
decoders that share a common prior* (frozen trunk + planner common
information). Anchors carry the common information (§7); dynamic notes carry
rate-accounted innovations (§5, §6); messages are shaped for the delay (§6);
commits are certified hypothesis tests with guaranteed retry progress (§8);
and all reads are lattice merges so training and runtime are the same
function (§4.1).

Notation: K streams; blocks of τ=32 tokens; reveal delay Δ (blocks); note
width d_notes; per-stream block count M = L/(Kτ) for total output length L;
residual inter-stream rate ρ (bits/block not predictable from the plan);
dependency-chain depth D; per-block abort probability p.

### Propositions (each becomes a pre-registered curve or bound)

- **P1 — Capacity bound (§5).** For any note message U with H(U) ≤ R bits,
  the dependency-span log-loss reduction it enables satisfies
  E[log p(Y|C,U) − log p(Y|C)] ≤ I(U;Y|C) ≤ R. With s-subvector VQ notes of
  codebook size C_vq: per-block delivered information ≤ K·s·ln C_vq nats.
  Violation ⇒ leakage (dataset contamination or side channel), by theorem.
- **P2 — Wyner decomposition (§7).** Coordination information =
  C_W(X_1..X_K) (Wyner common information, paid once at prompt time via
  anchors) + Σ_{i≠j} I(X_i; X_j | W) (residuals, paid on the bus). Required
  bus rate falls monotonically as the planner approaches the common-information
  extractor; the anchor-vs-notes benefit split is measurable (2×2).
- **P3 — Delay bound and its cancellation (§6).** For block states mixing
  along the stream, usable information of a stale summary decays:
  I(note_m; Y_{m+Δ}) ≤ I(S_m; S_{m+Δ}). A forecast-targeted note recovers the
  gap up to prediction residual: ΔCE(Δ) ≥ ΔCE(0) − ε_pred(Δ), and ε_pred
  shrinks as the plan makes streams predictable — delay tolerance is a
  *function of planner quality* (links P2→P3).
- **P4 — Staleness anomaly law and certifier spec (§8).** Δ-delayed reads
  implement snapshot isolation with bounded staleness Δ·τ tokens; the only
  admitted anomaly class is write skew = duplicate/contradictory slot claims
  within the Δ-window. Expected duplication rate ≈ C(K,2)·Δ·q²/S for q
  claims/block over S slots. A first-committer-wins certifier over declared
  claim vectors within the Δ-window is sound and complete for this class.
- **P5 — Progress theorem (§8).** Under greedy decoding, a retry whose note
  window is a subset of the aborted attempt's regenerates the identical block:
  livelock with probability 1 (the current repo's behavior). If rollback
  rewinds and *expands* the read set to include the conflicting sibling claim
  and the conditioned decoder avoids the visible conflict with probability
  ≥ β > 0, retries are geometric: E[retries] ≤ 1/β, wasted tokens ≤ τ/β.
- **P6 — Speedup law (§0.2, §11).**
  T_PDT = (L/K)/(1−p) + Δ·τ·D + t_plan, so
  S(L,K) = L / T_PDT → K(1−p) as L→∞, with crossover
  L* = K(1−p)(ΔτD + t_plan)/(K(1−p)−1) below which sequential wins. In
  Gunther-USL form S(K) = K/(1+σ(K−1)+κK(K−1)), PDT's coherency coefficient
  κ ∝ d_notes/τ is orders of magnitude below full-KV sharing's κ (which grows
  with sibling context length); with an abort hazard p(Δ) = 1−e^{−ρΔ} there is
  a unique interior optimum Δ*. Fit two constants on a calibration grid,
  predict held-out cells.

## 4. Phase 0 — Evidentiary Foundations (blocks all claims)

Nothing in §5–§8 is publishable while training and runtime disagree about
what a stream can see. Phase 0 is correctness plumbing with theorems.

### 4.1 CRDT Notes Bus — reads as lattice merges

**Defect class killed.** Four audited defects are one disease — the read
depends on delivery order and window composition, not on *which* notes were
delivered: (i) runtime one-shot visibility (`window.py` filters
`snap.version <= last_seen`, so each note is visible for exactly one token)
vs training persistent visibility; (ii) training `_visible_notes` ignores the
consumer (`del consumer`) and truncates `visible[-max_snapshots:]` in stream
order, silently evicting stream_0's anchor and block-0 write for every
consumer at K=3, max_snapshots=4; (iii) runtime `per_producer_limit =
max(1, 4//active) = 1` caps windows at 3 notes and can evict anchors;
(iv) write semantics diverge (training: masked mean over block tokens of
per-token SpeculationHead outputs; runtime: last token only).

**Change.** Bus state becomes a keyed LWW-map CRDT: per producer,
(version, note) with merge = per-producer version-max (associative,
commutative, idempotent ⇒ join-semilattice). SNC reads the merged fixed-size
K-slot state — one slot per producer, plus anchors — instead of a mutable
FIFO window. Training and runtime call the *same* read function of the
delivered-note set; the only remaining difference is the Δ delivery policy,
now auditable in isolation.

- `src/pdt/runtime/dnb_bus.py`: keyed LWW-map (Snapshot.metadata already
  carries what's needed — attachment point 10 of the audit).
- `src/pdt/runtime/window.py`: merged-state read replaces the last-seen
  filter, per-producer quotas, and truncation.
- `src/pdt/training/trainer.py::_visible_notes`: deleted; calls the shared
  read function.
- Write op unified (choose the attention-pooled write of §6 for both paths):
  `src/pdt/sidecar/heads/speculation.py`,
  `src/pdt/runtime/orchestrator.py::_emit_note_snapshot`.

**Theory.** Shapiro–Preguiça–Baquero–Zawirski (2011): join-semilattice state +
inflationary updates + merge=⊔ ⇒ Strong Eventual Consistency — replicas that
delivered the same update set are in identical states regardless of order,
duplication, timing. Corollary: train/test visibility divergence is
eliminated *by construction*, machine-checkably.

**Why novel.** Group Think/Hogwild! coordinate through order-*dependent*
RoPE-indexed shared caches — the opposite design point; LeaP injects routed
text with no invariance guarantee. Permutation-invariant aggregation is
standard ML, but no decoding-coordination work ties the aggregator to a
lattice and claims SEC as a machine-checkable ablation guarantee.

**Pre-registered test.** Three adversarial delivery schedules at eval
(random reorder; every note delivered 3×; truncate to most-recent per
producer): CRDT model's dependency-CE varies ≤1% across schedules (the
current model varies ≥10%); train-vs-runtime CE gap on dependency spans
shrinks ≥50%; per-stream CE spread from the anchor-eviction asymmetry <2%.

### 4.2 Addressed Notes + the Source-Swap Null Theorem

**Defect.** Notes carry no producer identity or recency; SNC softmax has no
abstain option. Small theorem with a large consequence: SNC's
softmax(qKᵀ)V consumes only {notes, mask} (producers/versions are built in
`window.py` then discarded), so the readout is **exactly invariant under
permutation of which sibling produced which note**. The pre-registered
source-swap intervention is therefore *provably incapable* of detecting
source binding — the contract as written is partially vacuous, not weak.
Repairing one's own falsification contract with a proof is itself a
contribution (no verified rival runs any causal-identification analysis of
its coordination channel).

**Change.**
- `src/pdt/sidecar/snc.py`: learned producer-ID and lag embeddings summed
  into key (optionally value) projections, sourced from the CRDT slot
  identity via a new `LayerRuntimeContext` field
  (`src/pdt/trunk/instrumentation.py`); append a learned null note n_∅ to
  every window so attention can abstain — the frozen scalar gate becomes
  content-conditioned per-token gating g·(1−α_∅(t)), which also mitigates the
  forced-mixing degradation on nondependency spans.
- `src/pdt/runtime/counterfactuals.py` + `src/pdt/cli/ablate.py`: add the
  producer-permutation condition (a built-in negative control), the
  parameter-matched **self-only** condition, and replace text-level
  cosine/ROUGE deltas with dependency-span ΔCE — the evaluation the contract
  already promises but the runner never computes.

**Theory.** Causal mediation with architectural identification: with the
trunk frozen and the prompt shared, the note is the *only* causal path from
sibling state to receiver output, so the natural indirect effect through the
bus is point-identified by mediator (note) substitution — sequential
ignorability holds by construction, not assumption. Total effect decomposes
as capacity (self-only vs gate-zero) + content NIE (real vs norm-scramble) +
source binding (provably zero before embeddings; measurable after). MAC

## 5. Measurement Theory — Bus Capacity Accounting *(load-bearing)*

**Corrected idea.** Turn the falsifiability contract into channel arithmetic
without identifying arbitrary model CE with mutual information. Generic
dependency-span CE reduction is a causal utility score. On the known-entropy
relay, whole-payload decoding gives a Barber--Agakov lower bound that can be
checked against the explicit channel rate. Dynamic notes now use
straight-through **product VQ (s=4 subvectors × 256 codes = 32 bits per
note)**. The bus transports only those indices and reconstructs the float
tensor from the checkpoint-shared codebook. Anchors have their own one-time
budget (16 slots × log₂ 8192 = 208 bits).

**Headline metric.** On the identified relay only, bus efficiency **η =
valid delivered-information lower bound / transmitted nats ∈ [0,1]**. Keep
generic LM-CE effects in a separate table. PDT's 32-bit messages can then be
compared with LeaP's ~10³–10⁴-bit text summaries and Hogwild's
~10⁶-bit-equivalent KV per block without pretending every CE delta is Shannon
information. The bound doubles as the
memorization alarm (kill criterion 2): ΔCE > bit budget is impossible via
the bus, so any violation localizes leakage automatically.

**Rate ladder / D(R) curve.** Train with Matryoshka nested dropout over note
dim-prefixes so every prefix is a usable code (successive refinement,
Equitz–Cover); sweep d_notes ∈ {8,16,32,64,128,256} for the D(R) curve —
the quantitative artifact reviewers will demand next to full-KV baselines.
Optional phase-2: a small rate-controller head allocating per-block width by
predicted dependency density (reverse water-filling; allocation
R_m = max(0, ½log(σ_m²/λ)) with σ_m² estimated from per-block ablation CE
deltas). Quantized runtime traffic: 4 B/stream-block — two further orders of
magnitude under the 512 B continuous figure.

**Files.** `speculation.py` (+product-VQ, same straight-through pattern as
`planner.py`; commit/codebook loss terms cloned in `losses.py`);
`cli/ablate.py` (η report + bound-violation alarm); config swaps fixed
d_notes for a bus-budget knob.

**Novelty.** PaCoRe/LeaP report accuracy vs message length empirically but
never bound benefit by channel rate; no verified work does information
accounting on an inter-stream channel. Nearest methodological art is MDL /
V-information probing (Voita & Titov; Xu et al. 2020) — single-model
representations, never a coordination channel, never a pre-registered
falsification instrument. Cite neural distributed source coding (Whang et
al.) and Matryoshka (Kusupati et al.) as ingredients.

**Pre-registered.** (a) validity: bound never violated on held-out prompts;
(b) usefulness: η ≥ 0.05 (≥1.1 nats/block delivered through 32 bits);
(c) dominance: η ≥ 10× the per-bit efficiency of the matched text channel;
(d) D(R): ≥80% of the 256-d benefit at 64 dims. If quantization drives ΔCE
to ~0, the continuous bus's apparent carriage was artifact or leakage.

## 6. Channel Design — Coding for the Delay

### 6.1 Predictive (Smith) notes: encode where the sibling *will be*

A note written at block m is read during block m+1 — stale by exactly τ
tokens by construction — yet the SpeculationHead is trained (implicitly, via
the rollout) to summarize the block that just ended. Control theory's answer
to a known constant dead time is the Smith predictor: feed back a forecast of
the state at *consumption* time, removing the delay from the loop's
characteristic equation, with degradation bounded by model mismatch.

**Change.** Two-line loss: L_fc = ‖n_k^m − stopgrad(ρ(h_k^{m+Δ}))‖² — the
teacher-forced rollout already materializes block m+Δ, so the target is free
(`trainer.py::_train_step`, weight in `LossWeights`). Optionally split notes
[summary ‖ forecast] (128/128) so runtime shapes are unchanged. Add a
lag-sweep mode over Δ ∈ {0,1,2,4} (bus lag is already a config parameter) to
trace D(Δ).

**Theory (P3).** Usable information of a stale summary decays by data
processing along the stream's chain; a forecast target replaces staleness
decay with predictability, and predictability is what the planner
manufactures — anchors ARE a plan — formally linking delay tolerance to
planner quality. Cite Intention Sharing (ICLR-21, MARL predict-forward
messages — no rate-limited channel, no decoding) and delay-compensated async
SGD as spiritual neighbors.

**Why novel.** Every verified neighbor is zero-delay (Group Think, LACE per
token; Hogwild!/AsyncReasoning instant KV) or delay-oblivious (LeaP, ~4K-token
cadence, no staleness treatment). Delay-aware message design only becomes
well-posed at PDT's design point — a differentiator full-bandwidth rivals
cannot copy without first adopting the architecture.

**Pre-registered.** The ledger's P3 row, including the signature
*interaction* effect: the CE-vs-Δ slope shrinks ≥50% under forecast targets
(a uniform offset with unchanged slope falsifies the Smith mechanism);
next-block probe decodability +≥0.1 AUC.

### 6.2 Wyner–Ziv residual notes: don't transmit what the receiver knows

The receiver already holds strong side information about its sibling (shared
prompt, anchors, own partial generation), yet the SpeculationHead compresses
unconditionally. Wyner–Ziv: with decoder side information Y, achievable rate
R_WZ(D) < R_X(D) whenever I(X;Y) > 0 — and SNC is already structurally a WZ
decoder (it fuses the message with receiver state).

**Change.** Side-information predictor head g(anchors, receiver block-mean
hidden) → predicted note; SpeculationHead emits the whitened residual;
adversarial/contrastive probe penalizes I(note; receiver-predictable state)
(`speculation.py`, `losses.py`, mirrored in
`orchestrator.py::_emit_note_snapshot`).

**Pre-registered.** At d_notes=64, WZ-residual notes retain ≥90% of the
vanilla-256 dependency-CE reduction while a probe predicting the note from
receiver context drops R² ≥0.5 → ≤0.15. Schedule after §5/§7 (medium risk of
marginal effect at small scale).

**Why novel.** Interlat compresses temporally at full width, ignoring
receiver knowledge; LeaP transmits fixed text regardless of peer state. No
system in the landscape codes its channel conditionally on the decoder's
correlated state.

## 7. Decomposition Theory — the Planner as Common-Information Extractor *(load-bearing)*

**Idea.** Nothing currently forces anchors to carry what is *common* across
the K futures. Formalize the two-tier channel: anchors ≈ Wyner common
information W (the minimal broadcast making streams conditionally
independent); the dynamic bus carries only residual pairwise dependencies
(P2). Add L_CI = Σ_{i<j} Î(H_i; H_j | W) over per-stream block-mean hiddens
given quantized anchors (conditional InfoNCE or HSIC — the stream-classifier
machinery already computes the pools). This gives the VQ planner a defined
information-theoretic job — **decorrelate the streams** — which is exactly
the thesis's non-overlap requirement, and it is the formal statement of the
throughput regime's ρ.

**Change.**
- `losses.py` + `schemas.py`: L_CI with YAML weight; gradients reach
  `planner.py` slot_projector and codebook through the existing
  straight-through path.
- **Learned ownership**: replace fixed round-robin
  (`orchestrator.py::_default_ownership`,
  `trainer.py::_round_robin_ownership`) with learned soft slot-to-stream
  assignment in `plan_notes_proj.py` — the planner allocates slots to
  minimize residual dependence. (This also upgrades "planner decomposition"
  from a fixed partition to an actual learned decomposition — currently the
  weakest link in the symmetry-breaking story.)
- Evaluation: first-class **2×2 {anchors on/off} × {dynamic notes on/off}**
  reporting — the instrument that answers kill criterion 1.

**Why novel.** No verified work pre-seeds a shared multi-stream workspace at
all (Interlat's plan is a sequential one-to-one prefix; PaCoRe seeds the next
*round* with text; Group Think/Hogwild! divide labor emergently via prompt).
Nobody formalizes decomposition as common-information extraction with an
explicit conditional-independence objective, or ties planner quality to
required channel rate. Cite Wyner-CI representation learning as ingredient
art.

**Pre-registered.** Ledger P2 row: with L_CI, anchor-share of total
dependency-CE reduction +≥15 points; cross-stream redundancy (pairwise
ROUGE-L) −≥20% relative; total reduction not degraded beyond bootstrap CI.

## 8. Commit Protocol — Transactional Decoding *(load-bearing)*

One section, three merged results (SI certifier + SPRT gate + OCC progress),
because as separate claims they read as a grab-bag and as one protocol they
are the paper's second pillar. The audit found AgreementHead and CoverageHead
constructed but never called, a noise `sigmoid(hidden.mean())` runtime score,
and a rollback path that checkpointed before checking. Those untrained heads,
scores, and rollback behavior are now removed from the canonical mechanism;
commit control remains a separately gated future implementation (P5).

### 8.1 The afternoon experiment (run first — costs hours, validates the audit)

> **DOWNGRADED — see §17.** "On current checkpoints" is a false premise: the
> repo contains **zero** checkpoints. At random init `W_o = 0` makes the SNC
> delta identically zero, so retries are token-identical *trivially*, proving
> nothing. The Tier-0 form is a control-flow determinism trace plus a
> forced-gate sweep; the real β̂ is Tier-1-gated.

On current checkpoints: measure the fraction of retried blocks that are

≥N% of firing episodes entering ≥3 identical retries. This is a falsification
test of the audit itself and the cheapest experiment in the program.

### 8.2 Claims + certifier (P4)

Each note gains a 16-dim **claim vector** over the planner's 16 VQ slots
(SpeculationHead sub-head; carried in `Snapshot.metadata`). Block-boundary
check becomes first-committer-wins certification: abort iff claim overlap
with an earlier-committed in-window sibling claim exceeds θ. CoverageHead is
retrained as the claim head (it finally receives gradients and inference
use); supervision from the existing plan-slot alignment.

Theory: snapshot-isolation (Berenson et al. 1995; Fekete et al. 2005) — the
Δ-delayed bus admits exactly one anomaly class (write skew = duplicated/
contradictory claims within the Δ-window); the certifier rejects exactly this
conflict set. Duplication law C(K,2)·Δ·q²/S is the pre-registered fit.

### 8.3 SPRT gate (drift detection)

AgreementHead re-typed to a log-likelihood-ratio statistic over the note
window: H0 = streams conditionally independent given the plan (the planner's
invariant), H1 = redundant/contradictory. Train contrastively on negatives
the repo already manufactures (source-swap, scramble, anchor-swap from
`counterfactuals.py`). Accumulate LLR across blocks as Wald's SPRT with
pre-registered (α, β) error rates. Theory: Ahlswede–Csiszár (1986) testing
against independence — the achievable type-II exponent is capped by note rate
R, so gate power is provably bounded by bus capacity, unifying §8 with §5.

### 8.4 OCC progress (P5)

Rollback path in `orchestrator.py`: (1) rewind `last_seen_version` to the
checkpoint (trivial under the CRDT bus); (2) force-include all sibling notes
committed during the aborted attempt — in particular the conflicting claim;
(3) retry at temperature >0 (or with the conflicting claim injected as an
extra SNC key/value). `trainer.py` gains conflict-replay episodes (synthetic
abort mid-rollout; supervise toward a non-conflicting continuation) to train
β up. Gate threshold θ* from the existing ROC tooling at the Neyman–Pearson
operating point minimizing τ·FPR/(1−p) + λ·FNR·cost_escape.

**Why novel.** No verified rival has *any* abort semantics (Hogwild!, Group
Think, LeaP, LaneRoPE never abort; speculative decoding's verifier supplies
the correction token directly, single-stream). Nobody formulates drift/overlap
detection as rate-constrained testing against independence, derives the gate
spec from an isolation-level theorem, or states a progress/abort-rate
analysis for parallel decoding. PDT is uniquely positioned because the
counterfactual transforms already exist in-repo.

**Pre-registered.** Ledger P4+P5 rows.

## 9. Training Dynamics — Gate Opening as Continuation (bus-death prevention)

> **CORRECTED — see §15.** The two-factor model below understates the defect:
> the real path is a **three-factor** product (outer `notes_gate` × inner SNC
> gate × zero-init `W_o`), so the effective initial scale is σ(−4)² ≈ 3.24e−4,
> not 1.8e−2, and the fix must replace **both** gates. §15 supersedes the
> arithmetic here; the structure of the argument is unchanged and strengthened.

**Exact result (verified against `snc.py`):** the SNC delta is
W_o(σ(g)·context) with W_o and its bias zero-initialized and g=−4. At step 0,
∂L/∂g = 0 **exactly** (the gate gradient flows only through W_o=0), and the
gradient into every note-content parameter (speculation head, k/v
projections, planner) is exactly zero — the whole coordination pathway sits
on a multiplicative saddle whose escape is bottlenecked by W_o growing first
(plateau length ~1/σ(g)², per deep-linear escape-time analysis). The
dying-channel risk is structural, not a hyperparameter accident — and the
same zero-init-gated recipe is folklore across the field (Flamingo lineage)
with no published analysis.

**Change.** Replace the free learnable gate with continuation: delta =
s·RMSNorm(SNC_raw) with s ∈ [0,1] a *scheduled* parameter advanced by a
corrector criterion — only when the EMA of ‖∇L w.r.t. note-content params‖
exceeds a floor (`snc.py`, new `LayerRuntimeContext` field, `trainer.py`
corrector loop, `gate_schedule` config block). Normalization removes one
factor of the degenerate product; the schedule cannot learn its way back to
zero.

**Pre-registered.** Micro (runs on current code in minutes): ‖∇L into
speculation-head params‖ scales as σ(g)·‖W_o‖_F with log-log slope 1.0±0.15
per factor; ∂L/∂g numerically zero at step 0. Macro: across ≥10 seeds, free
gates end collapsed (σ(g)<0.05, no dependency-CE gain) in ≥30% of seeds;
continuation achieves the gain in ≥90%; ablating only the RMSNorm restores
bimodality.

## 15. CORRECTION to §9 — the path is a THREE-factor product

## 18. CORRECTION — information identification and the GPU execution gate

The July 15 audit correctly found the rate axis and the packed-decode
opportunity, but it promoted three useful intuitions into theorems too early.

First, for arbitrary model distributions `q_with_note` and `q_without_note`,
their paired cross-entropy difference is a **causal utility score**, not
automatically conditional mutual information. It contains two unequal model
approximation errors. The proposed rule "CE benefit above R bits implies
leakage" is therefore invalid on generic language spans. Keep the paired CE
metric, but enforce bit accounting only on a known-entropy discrete payload
decoded from an explicit finite message. The new diagnostic implements the
Barber--Agakov lower bound for that identified case.

Second, Wyner common information is an optimization over auxiliary variables
that render sources conditionally independent; it is not additively completed
by a sum of pairwise conditional mutual informations. For K>2, pairwise sums
both double-count shared structure and miss synergy. The planner may still be
trained to reduce the *optimal* residual communication rate at a fixed plan
budget, but the old displayed decomposition must not appear as an equality.

Third, causal independence is necessary but insufficient for speed. The actual
hardware primitive is a packed matrix multiply over K frontier rows, which can
reuse frozen weight traffic. That primitive is now implemented with one
batch-shaped cache and verified on the real 4B trunk on MPS. Qwen3's private
GQA cache traffic still grows as `K*L`; a full-KV rival grows as `K^2*L`.
Thus length first amortizes planning/prefill, then increases cache pressure.
The roofline numbers remain lower bounds until the packed path is compared
against matched baselines on CUDA.

These corrections strengthen the publishable object: the paper should present
two independently calibrated instruments—a strict 32-bit information channel
and a packed-decode hardware path—instead of inferring either one from model CE
or token-round counts. Both instruments now exist; trained causal utility and
CUDA speed remain the decisive evidence gates.

The matched negative control is also now structural rather than post-hoc. A
`self_only` run owns an independent checkpoint and optimizer trajectory while
retaining the bus model's trainable tensor shapes, curriculum, loss, data, and
schedule. Its attention receives only the target stream's prompt tail and
delay-eligible target-history tail; it has no API through which sibling notes
can enter. The first CUDA gate is therefore operational rather than a design
gate: run the two-update optimizer probe, then launch matched 32-example bus
and self-only fits and apply the strict recovery comparison.
